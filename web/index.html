Add your wallets in Railway

In this service’s Variables, add CREATOR_PRIVATE_KEY_1 with your wallet’s base58 private key or 64-byte keypair JSON. Public addresses alone are rejected. Add CREATOR_PRIVATE_KEY_2 for another wallet, up to 50. An optional CREATOR_LABEL_1 names the first wallet. Redeploy after changes.

Creator keys remain on the Railway server and authorize only the selected launch settings. They are never returned to this page. Use dedicated wallets and keep Railway access private.

03

Execution

Initial buys are limited to 0.25 SOL total, before slippage, network fees and rent. Tips are limited to 0.005 SOL. Each wallet signs its own transaction; the last buyer pays the tip.
